# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming, respectful place to learn and collaborate. Participation in CemuX spaces—including issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions—comes with a responsibility to treat others with respect.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports a positive environment include:

- Showing patience and respect for different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback graciously.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work rather than people.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when mistakes occur.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project or its community in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/medo-iq), requesting a private contact route if needed. Please include enough context to understand the concern, and avoid sharing unrelated private information.

The maintainer will review reports and may remove or edit harmful content, issue a warning, or restrict participation where appropriate. The maintainer will consider the nature and impact of the behavior and may take further action when warranted. This project does not promise a particular response time.
